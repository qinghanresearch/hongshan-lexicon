Four expressions, not simply before and after

前红山, 红山前, 红山后, and 后红山 reverse similar elements but should not be reduced to two temporal labels. In particular, 红山后 can involve the place-name Hongshanhou. Treating every 后 as “after” erases a distinction the author makes.

Proposed distinctions

Chinese Dossier's proposal Relationship emphasized
前红山 pre-Hongshan A preceding stage
红山前 proto-Hongshan A proposed formative or immediate antecedent relationship
红山后 Hongshan proper (Hongshanhou) The eponymous site and Hongshan proper in this reading
后红山 post-Hongshan Subsequent to Hongshan, not its internal late phase

Why proto- requires care

Pre- primarily expresses temporal precedence; proto- can add a formative relationship. The first does not establish the second. This lexicon acknowledges an editorial inference in assigning particular archaeological cultures to the first two labels: Su did not define every correspondence explicitly.

This table is therefore an interpretive translation proposal, not an independently demonstrated four-stage chronology. The unverified BP correspondence table is not reproduced here.

Translation and usage

Retain the four Chinese expressions at first mention and explain the place-name reading of Hongshanhou. Where an immediate-precursor relationship is uncertain, identify the inference carried by proto- rather than making the translation more definite than its source.

Further verification

Compare the exact editions, pages, and contexts of Su's relevant writings. Do not assume the expressions have identical meanings in every text merely because a compact reference table is convenient.
