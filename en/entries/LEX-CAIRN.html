cairn：石堆与石冢

What does cairn emphasize?

Cairn emphasizes a heap or construction of stones; its function depends on context. The Dartmoor survey uses the term in its funerary glossary but also records clearance cairns under land clearance. A cairn is therefore not necessarily a grave. [1]

Applying it to jishizhong 积石冢

Where the funerary context is established, cairn can be used with an explanation. Burial cairn or stone burial mound may be clearer to general readers. Continue to distinguish the stone feature as a whole from individual interments.

Translation and usage

石堆 may suit a general stone feature; 石冢 or 积石冢 may suit a confirmed funerary structure, depending on the object. Do not assign every cairn to a specifically Hongshan monument type.

A methodological reminder

Names organize observations but do not determine function. Distinguishing burial monuments from clearance heaps requires contextual and structural evidence.
