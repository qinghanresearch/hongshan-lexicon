An official formulation and its versions

This entry records the five features of Chinese civilization as continuity, innovativeness, unity, inclusiveness, and peacefulness, under the heading the five prominent features of Chinese civilization.

Why versions matter

The editorial dossier records variation across Xinhua, the English edition of Qiushi, China Daily, and texts associated with China's mission to the United Nations. An earlier wording can be relevant when quoting a document from a particular date. Later terminology should not silently replace the wording of a historical quotation.

Translation and usage

When quoting an official document, consult and cite its own official English version. The formulation displayed here reproduces the selection in the editorial dossier; it is not a claim that every institutional source currently uses identical wording.

The terms five distinctive features, defining features, creativity, consistency, and originality remain searchable as recorded variants. Their presence in the index does not make them freely interchangeable within a fixed quotation.

Scope of editorial responsibility

Explanatory discussion of civilization and reproduction of an official formulation are different tasks. Rules devised for translating archaeological analysis should not be used to rewrite an official quotation.
