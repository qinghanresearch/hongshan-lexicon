A descriptive name for an uncertain function

The Chinese name describes a cylindrical jade object with an oblique opening. The recommended English term is hoof-shaped jade hoop. Related Chinese names include 马蹄形玉箍 and 玉箍形器.

Hoof and hoop do different jobs

Hoof-shaped describes the resemblance used in naming the form. Hoop supplies an object-class label. Neither word establishes how the object was worn, mounted, displayed, or used. Such functional proposals require evidence beyond the name.

The editorial dossier records international catalogue usage of hoof-shaped and proposes replacing the earlier wording “Horseshoe-Shaped Jade Hoops.” A hoof and a horseshoe are not the same referent: the latter can suggest a particular open, curved shape that may mislead readers about this object.

Translation and usage

Use hoof-shaped jade hoop for the agreed descriptive label in this edition. When visual precision matters, add a description of the oblique opening and cylindrical form. Do not silently convert the name into a claim that the object was a bracelet or headdress component.
