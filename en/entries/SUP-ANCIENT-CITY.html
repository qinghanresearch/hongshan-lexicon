Ancient cities and their definition

古城

Preserve a broad title while explaining the author’s more limited definition.

Why ancient city needs a contextual definition

Su Bingqi's sequence “ancient cultures, ancient cities, and ancient states” has a deliberate conceptual rhythm. Yet his definition of 古城 concerns towns and settlements at the earliest differentiation between urban and rural forms, rather than cities or metropolises in the ordinary sense.

The translation problem

Using cities without explanation in the definition can suggest a mature urban form. Replacing the title throughout with towns changes its conceptual structure. The solution is to preserve both the broad heading and the limiting definition.

Translation and usage

This lexicon retains ancient cities in titles or the three-part formulation, while using an explanation such as towns and settlements in the sense of the earliest urban–rural differentiation in the definition. A note can clarify the relationship at first mention.

This is a decision for Su's argument, not a universal translation for every Chinese site called 古城. For individual sites, specify chronology, structure, and the author's criteria for identifying an urban settlement.

A transferable principle

Make an author's conceptual tension visible rather than smoothing it away. Readers need to understand how the word is being used before they can assess the wider argument about civilization.
