Translation ethics

Make translation judgments visible

Distinguish naming, observation, and interpretation. Preserve the author's uncertainty, provisional labels, and attribution of disputed views. A translation must not silently turn a proposal into an established conclusion.

Respect historical contexts

Retain names and classifications appropriate to the historical text. Later identifications may be explained in notes rather than attributed retrospectively to the author. Official quotations follow their corresponding published versions.

Citation and responsibility

Do not invent references, page numbers, or claims of verification. Distinguish material consulted directly, material reported through editorial sources, and editorial inference. AI-assisted prose requires the same evidential scrutiny as other writing; fluency confers no scholarly authority.

Limits of this edition

This page summarizes the reading edition's principles from the project's statement of translator responsibility. New exposition and English prose await editorial review. It does not certify final verification of every historical claim, translation, or object identification.
